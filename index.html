Method

1. The ListEvery property on the UNESCO World Heritage List1, inscribed since 1978 under the 1972 World Heritage Convention. Each site is Cultural, Natural or Mixed, and carries the criteria (i)–(x) it was inscribed under. Sites the World Heritage Committee has placed on the List of World Heritage in Danger are drawn in red, overriding their category color. Total sites displayed: —.

2. Geographic dataCoordinates come from the official UNESCO record for each property. Serial properties — a single inscription made of several separate components, such as the 758 parts of the Frontiers of the Roman Empire — publish one point per component; those sites are placed on their main component. those components are clustered by distance and a site can receive several label points, but secondary clusters are kept only when they hold enough of its components. Points are colored by category, highlighting where cultural and natural heritage concentrate.

3. Popularity and imagesName size and weight come from Wikipedia pageviews (human traffic only) over the last completed year. More-viewed sites appear earlier; zooming in reveals less-viewed ones. Photos come from the UNESCO photo gallery published with each property record.
